# Baseline vs with-layers: the paired comparison

> ### Read this before quoting any number below
>
> **Group medians carry weight. Single-question figures do not.** Run-to-run variance on the same question, under identical conditions, is about **40% on tool calls and 20% on seconds**, measured over 140 questions that were run twice. Q078 moved from 274.8s to 109.5s between two runs of itself; Q063 moved from 275.0s to 644.5s. Any claim about an individual question needs repeats before it means anything.
>
> **Correct carries about 3 points of judge noise.** 114 measurements were judged twice; the judge agreed with itself on **92.1%**. Every disagreement was adjacent (6 Y->P, 2 P->Y, 1 P->N) and **none was Y->N**, so it never reversed a correct answer into a wrong one. Treat a few points' difference between two Correct figures as noise.
>
> **One question in the set is known broken.** I22 refers to another question by id, which no session can resolve, so its N/N/P is a defect in the question rather than a result. Excluding it moves group I from 76% to 79% baseline and 93% to 95% with layers, and the conclusion is unchanged. See `QUESTION-DEFECTS.md`.
>
> **Route now covers the whole set.** Groups G and I define no expected route in v1; the route overlay supplies them as an overlay, leaving the frozen set untouched. One caveat on reading group G's baseline figure: its route is "grep the artefacts and find nothing", so a baseline with no artefacts scores 0 on route by construction, however good its answer.

## Where the layers earn their keep, and where they do not

Ranked by speedup:

- **E Blueprint blast radius** - 5.16x
- **H Engine API surface** - 3.09x
- **D Comments and documentation** - 2.70x
- **B Inheritance and ancestry** - 2.38x
- **F Negation: absence of replication** - 2.26x
- **A Structure: definition sites, property types** - 2.06x
- **C Reflection: specifiers, replication** - 1.97x
- **G Traps: non-existence and ambiguity** - 1.73x
- **I Build, tooling and procedure** - 1.63x

The spread across groups is the finding, not the overall number. A group where the layers barely help is either a question-design problem (the answer was reachable without them) or a routing problem (`CLAUDE.md` does not name the artefact for that question shape). Both are worth chasing; the second is the one the standing lesson in `BASELINE-ARM-SPEC.md` warns about.
