- Doc: Creates the seed Blueprints the benchmark needs, so the Blueprint questions in group E and F have something real to run against. Blueprints are binary assets and cannot be hand written, so they are generated here rather than committed by hand.  The important part is that the graphs contain real UK2Node_CallFunction nodes pointing at C++ UFunctions. That is what makes BP_AccelDemoCharacter a genuine caller of ApplyDamage, invisible to clangd and to ripgrep, which is the whole point of the exercise.
